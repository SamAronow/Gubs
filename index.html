How a round goes

  1. Draw one card, or skip the draw — but never two turns running.
  2. Play as many cards as you like. Gubs and Barricades stay on the table; Tools and Hazards are discarded once used.
  3. End your turn holding no more than eight cards.

Events fire the instant they are drawn. When the third letter of G–U–B comes off the deck the game stops dead, and whoever has the most Free and Protected Gubs on their side wins.
